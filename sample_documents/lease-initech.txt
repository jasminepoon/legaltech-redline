COMMERCIAL LEASE

This Commercial Lease is made between Harbor Point Properties LP (the "Landlord") and Initech Software Co. (the "Tenant") for the premises at 100 Example Way, Suite 400 (the "Premises").

1. Term. The Tenant shall lease the Premises for a term of thirty-six (36) months, beginning on the Commencement Date.

2. Rent. The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $8,500 to the Landlord on the first day of each month. Rent for any partial month is prorated.

3. Security Deposit. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to two (2) months of rent. The Landlord shall return the security deposit within thirty (30) days after the Tenant vacates the Premises, less any amounts applied to unpaid rent or damage.

4. Use. The Tenant shall use the Premises only for general office purposes.

5. Maintenance. The Landlord shall maintain the roof, structure, and common areas. The Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises in good condition.

6. Notices. Notices to the Landlord must be sent to the Landlord's office. Notices to the Tenant must be sent to the Premises.

7. Default. If the Tenant fails to pay rent within ten (10) days after it is due, the Landlord may give written notice of default. If the Tenant does not cure the default within thirty (30) days after notice, the Landlord may terminate this Lease.
